07 — Backtest

Value in BTC (1.0 = buy & hold)
Starts with 1 BTC and no cash. 0.1% fee per trade, no tax. Each tier measures the move from the lowest price (sells) or highest price (buys) inside its own day window. A tier fires when its rule starts matching, and can fire again after the rule stops matching and then matches again. Amounts use the midpoint of ranges: S1 0.8%, S2 2%, S4 8%, Model 1 S6 16%, B2 65%. Model 2: S6 14%, S7 18%.

08 — What the test says

  • How tiers count now: sells measure today's price against the lowest intraday low in the tier's window (0 = today), buys against the highest intraday high. A tier fires once when the move reaches its band (or goes past it), and re-arms after the move falls back below.
  • Model 1 still gives up BTC over the long run: from 2017 it ends with 0.37 BTC-worth against 1.0 for buy & hold, from 2022 0.76. In the 2025–26 fall it is ahead: 1.33 since Jan 2025.
  • Model 2 is similar or a bit better: 0.38 from 2017, 0.64 from 2020, 1.48 since 2025.
  • 55-day SMA check helps Model 1 most: 0.65 BTC-worth from 2017, 0.87 from 2022.
  • Trend gate keeps the most BTC long-term (Model 1: 0.77 from 2017, 0.91 from 2020) but has not traded since 2021.
  • Using intraday lows/highs plus "fire on reach" improved both models in almost every period versus the old closing-price bands.
