07 — Backtest

Value in BTC (1.0 = buy & hold)
Starts with 1 BTC and no cash. 0.1% fee per trade, no tax. Each tier measures the move from the lowest price (sells) or highest price (buys) inside its own day window. A tier fires when its rule starts matching, and can fire again after the rule stops matching and then matches again. Amounts use the midpoint of ranges: S1 0.8%, S2 2%, S4 8%, Model 1 S6 16%, B2 65%. Model 2: S6 14%, S7 18%.

08 — What the test says

  • Model 1 (corrected) gives up BTC over the long run: from 2017 it ends with 0.20 BTC-worth against 1.0 for buy & hold, from 2020 0.38. In the 2025–26 fall it is ahead: 1.40 BTC-worth since Jan 2025.
  • Model 2 behaves about the same: 0.18 from 2017, 0.29 from 2020, 1.40 since 2025. Its extra tiers (S5–S7) rarely fire because those bands need very big rallies.
  • 55-day SMA check helps Model 1 the most: 0.44 BTC-worth from 2017, 0.60 from 2020, and a smaller worst drop.
  • Trend gate: Model 2 gated ends with 1.79 BTC-worth from 2017, beating buy & hold, but from only 36 trades around the 2017 and 2021 tops. It has not traded since 2021. Treat it as fragile.
  • Bands matter: a tier fires only when the move lands inside its band. If BTC jumps past a band in one day (e.g. from +7% to +11%), that tier is skipped.
